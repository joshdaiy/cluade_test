description: A pre-done reality check — surface what I'm unsure of and what I might be missing
---

Before we call this done, stop and answer these three questions honestly about
the current state of the work / situation. This is a self-check, not reassurance:
resist the urge to sound finished. If an answer is "nothing," say why you're
confident, don't just assert it.

$ARGUMENTS

Answer each one directly, one at a time:

1. **What am I least confident about right now?** Name the specific part of the
   work, claim, or decision you'd be least surprised to find is wrong. Say what
   would make you more sure.
2. **What's the biggest thing I'm missing about the situation right now?** Step
   back from the immediate task. What context, constraint, or angle have I not
   accounted for that could change the whole picture?
3. **What don't I realize?** The blind spot — the thing I'm not even thinking to
   ask about. If you can name it, name it. If you can only gesture at where it
   might be, do that.

End with a one-line verdict: is this actually ready, or is there something to
resolve first?
