Grill me on the following text. This is an interrogation, not a review.

$ARGUMENTS

Rules:
- Ask **one** pointed question at a time, then STOP and wait for my answer.
- Start with the load-bearing weakness — the thing that, if it collapses, takes
  the rest down with it. Usually that's the thesis or the strongest counterargument
  I'm dodging.
- Do not accept a restatement of the claim as evidence for the claim. Call it out.
- If my answer is good, say so briefly and move to the next weakest point.
- If my answer is weak or hand-wavy, press again on the same point before moving on.
- After ~5–8 exchanges, summarize: what held up, what didn't, and the single most
  important thing to fix before this goes anywhere.

Do not write the fix for me. Make me find it.
