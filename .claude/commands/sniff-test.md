Run a sniff test on the following. The question is simple: **does this sound like
a real person — specifically me — or does it sound performed, generic, or written
by an adult / an AI?**

$ARGUMENTS

Steps:
1. If a `voice/` folder exists in this project, read my writing samples there
   first and use them as the baseline for how I actually sound (rhythm, word
   choice, humor, sentence length). Treat class writing as my *voice fingerprint*,
   not as the target formality — admissions essays are more personal.
2. Go line by line and flag anything that trips the sniff test:
   - **Doesn't sound like me** — quote it, and say what about my samples it clashes with.
   - **Performed / trying to impress** — grand abstractions, SAT words I'd never say aloud.
   - **AI / adult polish** — suspiciously balanced, over-smooth, generically "essay-ish."
   - **Cliché topic or phrase** — worn frames (winning game, mission trip,
     grandparent → "resilience," "I want to help people") or stock phrasing.
3. For each flag, ask: "Would you actually say this out loud to a friend?" Make me
   answer honestly.

Do NOT rewrite anything in my voice — that defeats the purpose. Point, name the
problem, and make me fix it in my own words. End with the one sentence that sounds
*most* like me (so I know my target) and the one that sounds least like me.
