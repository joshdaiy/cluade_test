Steelman the opposition to my argument.

$ARGUMENTS

1. State my thesis back to me in one sentence so we agree on the target.
2. Build the **strongest, most charitable** version of the case against it — the
   argument a smart, informed opponent would actually make, not a strawman.
3. Point to the specific places in my piece that this counterargument exposes as
   weak, unsupported, or unaddressed.
4. Then ask me: does my essay need to (a) answer this head-on, (b) narrow its
   claim to avoid it, or (c) concede it and argue anyway? Make me choose and
   defend the choice.

The goal is that I never get blindsided by an objection I could have seen coming.
Do not resolve it for me — surface it and make me handle it.
