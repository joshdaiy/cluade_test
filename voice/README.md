# voice/ — your writing samples

Drop your own past writing here so the coach knows what *you* sound like. This is
**reference material, not training** — nothing here changes the model. It's read as
a baseline so the coach can tell when your draft drifts away from your real voice.

## What to add

- A **curated handful** (roughly 3–8 pieces), not everything you've ever written.
- Favor anything **personal or narrative** — reflections, personal essays, a college
  essay you liked, writing where your voice comes through.
- Analytical / lit-analysis class essays are still useful as a *voice fingerprint*
  (rhythm, diction, humor), but they're usually more formal than admissions essays
  want — so they show how you *sound*, not how the essay should *read*.
- Plain text or markdown is easiest. One file per piece, any filename.

## How it's used

- **Diagnostic only.** The coach uses these to flag sentences that stop sounding
  like you, and to point you back toward your own patterns when you're stuck.
- **Never to generate or imitate you.** The essay has to be yours — samples make the
  coach better at *holding you to your voice*, not at faking it.

`/sniff-test` and `/ship-check` (gate 1) automatically read this folder when it has
content.
